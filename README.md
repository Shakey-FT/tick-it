# Tick It

A simple checklist that works on your phone or computer. You can keep as many lists as you like, and there's nothing to sign up for or install.

**👉 Open it: https://shakey-ft.github.io/tick-it/**

## What you can do

- **Keep several lists** for groceries, packing, chores or anything else. Each one shows how many items are done.
- **Add, tick, edit and delete items.** Type in the box and press **Enter** or the round **+**. Tap an item's text to change it. On a phone, swipe an item left to delete it. On a computer, use the **×** that appears when you point at an item.
- **Add several items at once.** Type them with commas, like `milk, eggs, bread`, and tap **Add as 3 items**. Pressing Enter still adds it as one item, and numbers like `1,5 kg` stay together.
- **Suggestions as you type.** Start typing and Tick It suggests things you've added before, so "mi" offers **Milk**. Tap one to add it. If it's already on the list and ticked, tapping it unticks it instead of adding a copy. What it remembers stays on your device, and you can turn this off or clear it in Settings (with an Undo if you change your mind).
- **Headings.** Split a list into groups, like aisles or bags: tap the add box, tap **Heading** underneath it, and type. (Starting a line with `#`, like `# Fruit`, works too.) New items go under the last heading, ticked items move to the bottom of their own group, and each heading shows how many of its items are done.
- **Sub headings.** Groups inside a heading, like the flights on each day: tap **Sub heading** under the add box, or tap an existing heading and choose **Make it a sub heading**. (`##` at the start of a line works too.) A heading counts everything in its sub headings too.
- **Paste a whole list at once.** Paste lines copied from a note or message and each line becomes its own item. Bullets, numbers and checkboxes are tidied away, and items marked `[x]` or ☑ arrive ticked. Lines like `# Fruit` or `Fruit:` become headings. A heading with other headings straight under it, like a day with its flights, holds them as sub headings.
- **Find things.** In a long list, tap the magnifying glass to show only the items that match. On the home screen, **Find a list or item** also searches inside your lists and opens the list showing just the matches.
- **Share a list** (⋯ → **Share list**):
  - **Send a link:** whoever gets it taps the link to add the list to their own Tick It, with its headings and ticks. The list travels inside the link itself, so it's never uploaded anywhere.
  - **Send as text:** a readable checklist for WhatsApp, Messages, email or anywhere else. It can be pasted straight into Tick It too.
- **Ticked items move to the bottom**, so what's left to do stays at the top. The tick draws in, then after a moment the item glides down, so the row under your finger doesn't suddenly change. You can turn this off in Settings, and there's no motion if your phone has **Reduce motion** on.
- **List options (⋯)** at the top of each list: share it, pick a list colour, reorder items, untick all, clear done items, duplicate the list, or delete it.
- **Duplicate a list** to reuse it with everything unticked. Handy for packing lists and weekly routines. **Untick all** does the same for the list you're in.
- **Undo mistakes.** Deleting something shows an **Undo** button for a few seconds.
- **Move an item to another list.** On a phone, swipe it right. On a computer, use the arrow button that appears when you point at it. Pick a list, or start a new one with it, and there's an Undo.
- **Move and delete lists on the home screen.** On a phone, press and hold a list to drag it, or swipe it left to delete it. On a computer, point at a list for its drag handle and **×**. Deleting a list always offers Undo.
- **Pin lists to the top** of the home screen (⋯ → **Pin to top**). In Settings you can also sort the rest by **Recently used**.
- **Move items.** On a phone, press and hold an item, then drag it. Hold it near the top or bottom of the screen and the list scrolls for you. Or use ⋯ → **Reorder items** (or ⋯ → **Reorder lists** on the home screen) and drag the ⠿ handle.
- **Delete several lists at once:** on the home screen, tap **⋯ → Select lists**, tap the lists you want, then **Delete**.
- **Filter** to see everything, only what's left to do, or only what's done.
- **Opens instantly, and works offline.** Once you've opened it, it opens straight from your device, even with a poor signal or none at all. New versions download in the background, and Tick It offers to reload when one is ready.
- **Make it yours** in **Settings** (tap **⋯** on any screen, then **Settings**). A preview at the top shows each change as you make it.
  - **Colour:** 16 colours, 5 seasonal themes (Spring, Ocean breeze, Autumn, Winter and Festive), or **Custom** to pick any colour. Custom colours are adjusted slightly if needed so text stays easy to read.
  - **Style:** **Classic**, **Tinted** (the whole page takes on your colour), **Paper** (notebook paper with handwriting, unless you pick another font), **Minimal** (no cards, just fine lines) or **Soft** (rounder, airier rows).
  - **Font:** Modern, Rounded, Classic, Handwriting, or **Easy to read** (Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for people with low vision).
  - **Text size** from Small to Extra large, **high contrast**, **compact rows** and **round or square** tick boxes.
  - Light, Dark or **Match device**, with **true black** for OLED screens in dark mode.
  - Turn off the **confetti**, or on Android the little **buzz**, when you finish a list.
  - **About Tick It** at the bottom shows the version and what's new, and on a phone lets you see the tips again.
  - On a computer, the arrow keys move between choices and **Esc** goes back a step.
- **Give a list its own colour** (⋯ → **List colour**) so it stands out on the home screen: pick one of the colours, or **Custom** to choose any colour.
- **Back up and restore** your lists, or move them to another device. It reminds you if you haven't backed up in a while.

## How to use it

1. Open the link above.
2. Type an item in the box and press **Enter** or the round **+**.
3. Tap the box next to an item to tick it off.
4. Tap **‹ All lists** to see your lists, then the round **+** button in the bottom corner to start another. On a computer, it's **+ New list** at the top.

### Put it on your home screen

This adds a Tick It icon to your phone, and it opens full-screen like an app.

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

> **iPhone:** the home screen app keeps its own lists, separate from Safari. If you've already made lists in Safari, open the link in Safari, tap **⋯ → Backup & restore → Copy as text**, then paste it into **Backup & restore** in the home screen app. The app shows a reminder about this the first time you open it. On Android, your lists appear in both places.
>
> Links someone sends you open in Safari, not the home screen app. To put a shared list in the app, tap **Copy link** on the screen that opens, then paste the link into the **Add an item** box of any list in the app.

## Where your lists are saved

Your lists are saved **in your own browser, on your own device**. They're never sent anywhere, and nobody else can see them, including the person who shared the link with you.

This means:

- Your lists stay on the phone or computer where you made them. They don't sync between devices.
- **Clearing your browser data deletes them.** So do private or incognito windows, when you close them.
- Safari on iPhone may clear them if you don't open the page for about a week. Using the home screen app avoids this, and a backup keeps you safe either way.
- Tick It asks your browser to keep your lists rather than clear them when the device runs low on space. Most browsers decide this without asking you. **Backup & restore** tells you when the browser has agreed.

### Keep a backup

Tap **⋯** then **Backup & restore** on the **All lists** screen, or find it under **Your data** in Settings:

- **Download backup file** or **Copy as text** saves a copy of all your lists. Email it to yourself or keep it in a notes app.
- To restore, choose the backup file or paste the text, then pick **Add to my lists** or **Replace my lists**.

This is also how you move your lists to a new phone.

> Backups are plain, readable text, so don't keep passwords or other private details in your lists.

## Privacy

- No accounts, no tracking and no analytics.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google can see that the page was visited. Extra fonts only load if you choose them.
- Everything else stays on your device.

## About this project

The whole app is a single file, [`index.html`](index.html), plus [`sw.js`](sw.js), which keeps a copy on your device so it works offline. It's hosted free on GitHub Pages. To make your own copy, fork this repo and turn on **Settings → Pages** (branch `main`, folder `/root`).
