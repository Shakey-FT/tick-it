        About Tick It

        What’s new

        • The home screen shows your week: how many items you’ve ticked each day, and how that compares with last week. Tap a day to see its number. Counting starts now, and you can turn it off in Settings → Lists.
        • The home screen greets you with good morning, afternoon or evening and today’s date. “My lists” now labels your lists just above them.
        • The app is smaller, so it loads a little faster.
        • Pressing Enter on a phone keyboard now adds the item, heading or sub heading and keeps the keyboard up.
        • Lists can have a custom colour too: ⋯ → List colour → Custom. Dragging across the colours in the colour picker now works on phones.
        • Headings are bolder, and each one starts its own card. Sub headings sit in a soft pill of your theme colour, with a dot, and the items under them are set in a little. A sub heading with just one item shows a ✓ once it’s done, instead of a count.
        • Adding headings is easier: while you type in the add box, tap Heading or Sub heading underneath it.
        • Custom colour now works on phones: tap Custom and your phone’s colour picker opens.
        • Sub headings, for groups inside a heading, like the flights on each day. Find them under ⋯ → Add a sub heading, or tap a heading to switch it.
        • Pasting a list with a heading straight above other headings puts those headings inside it.
        • On the home screen, press and hold a list to move it, or swipe it left to delete it, with Undo. On a computer, point at a list for the × and a drag handle.
        • Tick It opens straight away, even on a poor connection. When there’s a new version, it offers to reload.
        • Moving an item by pressing and holding is smoother on phones: the list scrolls while you hold near the top or bottom, and letting go anywhere drops it in the nearest place.
        • Fixes: an edit is kept if you go back mid-edit or have Tick It open in two tabs, backups restore even if an item holds a Tick It link, Undo after pasting a list puts its name back, and very long lists are much quicker.
        • In Settings, the option you’ve chosen is easier to see, and there’s Undo for Forget past items.
        • On a computer, the arrow keys move between choices, and Esc goes back a step.

        Recently added

        • Custom colours, five fonts, text sizes, and the Minimal and Soft styles.
        • Move an item to another list, pin lists to the top, and add several items at once with commas.
        • Headings, Find, suggestions as you type, and sharing a list as a link.

        There’s no account and no adverts, and your lists never leave this device unless you share or back them up.

        Backup & restore

        Your lists are saved only in this browser. Keep a backup in case browser data gets cleared, or use one to move your lists to another phone or computer.
